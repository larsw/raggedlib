# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers use raggedlib to search, ask questions about, and read their local Calibre ebook libraries. Curators manage writable libraries and their metadata; library administrators also manage indexing and access.

## Product Purpose

Raggedlib makes a local Calibre library searchable and answerable, with answers grounded in cited passages. Success means readers can find and verify useful information without sending their books to an external service.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, unobtrusive. The interface should support focused library work without distracting from the books.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard patterns, decorative complexity, and controls that obscure library state or require unnecessary steps.

## Design Principles

- Keep reading and library-management workflows clear and direct.
- Make access, editability, and indexing state visible where relevant.
- Keep book content and user data local.
- Prefer familiar, consistent controls over novel interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls with keyboard and screen-reader support, maintain visible focus, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA.
